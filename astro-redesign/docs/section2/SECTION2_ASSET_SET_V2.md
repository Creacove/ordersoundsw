# Section 2 — Approved Asset Set V2

## Locked concept
**All the work around the music. Handled.**

This document supersedes the earlier Section 2 asset guidance wherever there is a conflict.

## Critical brand decision
The loose objects must feel like real working materials around an artist, not branded merchandise.

**Only the final bound stack may visibly carry the OrderSounds logo.**

Do not place the logo on:
- release booklet
- business document
- opportunities pass
- what's-next note
- any future loose paper prop

The final `handled-stack.webp` is intentionally the branded payoff: once the scattered work has been gathered and handled, it becomes Desk.

The exact logo source remains available at:
`/section2/ordersounds-logo-source.png`

Never redraw or approximate it.

## Motion implication
The loose stage should use the approved unbranded props. The final bound stack is the first and only moment in Section 2 where the OrderSounds mark becomes visible.

That is intentional.

The brand reveal should therefore feel earned:
1. career work appears as real unbranded working material;
2. it feels scattered but beautifully art-directed;
3. the material organizes;
4. the loose pieces disappear into the final handled bundle;
5. the final bundle reveals the OrderSounds logo on the black binding.

Do not add a separate logo animation. The logo appearing naturally on the final bundle is enough.

## Marketing representation
There is no approved standalone marketing prop in V2. Do not fall back to the rejected logo-heavy marketing sheet and do not invent a new one.

Marketing is still represented in the final stack artwork and in the overall concept. Build the loose choreography from the approved pieces only unless a new marketing prop is explicitly approved later.

## Quality
The WebP files are lossless exports of the approved transparent PNG masters to protect paper edges and small printed typography.

Do not recompress them to lower-quality WebP/JPEG during implementation.

## Everything else
The existing Section 2 design/motion brief remains valid:
- continue visually from the hero;
- semantic HTML headline on the left;
- physical DOM image layers on the right;
- GSAP ScrollTrigger;
- scattered → organized → handled;
- no SaaS cards;
- no AI visual language;
- no loops;
- desktop visual QA first, then intentional mobile/tablet compositions.
